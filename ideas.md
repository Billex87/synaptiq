## Idea 1 — "Dark Sanctum" (Chosen)

**Design Movement:** Dark Luxury Minimalism × Biophilic Noir

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep darkness as the primary canvas — near-black backgrounds with micro-texture
2. Accent lighting philosophy: teal-ice for cold, amber-ember for heat, crimson for light therapy
3. Generous negative space — sections breathe like a premium spa
4. Typography as architecture — large editorial letterforms anchor each section

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: `#0A0B0D` (near-black with a hint of cool blue-grey)
- Surface: `#111318` (elevated card surfaces)
- Cold accent: `#4ECDC4` / `#00B4D8` (icy teal — cold plunge)
- Heat accent: `#C9783A` / `#E8A45A` (amber ember — sauna)
- Light accent: `#C0392B` / `#E74C3C` (deep crimson — red light)
- Text primary: `#F0EDE8` (warm off-white — not harsh pure white)
- Text secondary: `#8A8A8A` (muted grey)
- Gold accent: `#B8965A` (luxury detail lines)

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-viewport hero with parallax depth
- Asymmetric section layouts — alternating left/right content blocks
- Horizontal scroll treatment for service cards on mobile
- Floating navigation bar with blur-glass effect
- Large section numbers as decorative typography (01, 02, 03...)

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin horizontal gold rule lines separating sections
2. Glowing orb/halo effects behind service imagery (matching service color)
3. Oversized italic serif display text for hero headlines

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered fade-up animations (subtle, not jarring)
- Hover states reveal service details with smooth opacity transitions
- CTA buttons use a thin border + subtle glow on hover

**Animation:**
- Hero: slow Ken Burns zoom on background image
- Section entries: staggered fade-up with 100ms delays
- Service cards: lift + glow on hover
- Navigation: blur-glass appears on scroll

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Cormorant Garamond` — italic, large, editorial (headlines)
- Body: `DM Sans` — clean, modern, readable (body text, UI)
- Accent: `Cormorant Garamond` small caps for section labels

## Idea 2 — "Arctic Zen"

**Design Movement:** Scandinavian Minimalism × Cryotherapy Clinical

**Core Principles:**
1. Ice-white and pale stone as primary palette
2. Ultra-thin typography with extreme tracking
3. Photography bleeds edge-to-edge with no borders
4. Silence between elements — extreme whitespace

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: `#F5F7F8` (pale ice white)
- Accent: `#1A3A4A` (deep navy)
- Cold: `#A8D8EA` (pale blue)
- Warm: `#D4A574` (sand)

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Brutalist grid with intentional asymmetry
- Full-bleed photography sections
- Minimal navigation — just logo + one CTA

**Signature Elements:**
1. Ultra-thin hairline borders
2. Large negative space sections
3. Monochrome photography with single color overlays

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover reveals color from monochrome
- Smooth page transitions

**Animation:**
- Fade only — no movement
- Slow image reveals on scroll

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Playfair Display` — thin weight
- Body: `Lato` — light weight

## Selected: **Idea 1 — "Dark Sanctum"**

This approach best matches the brand's luxury lifestyle positioning, calming vibe, and contrast therapy identity. The tri-color accent system (teal/amber/crimson) maps perfectly to the three hero services and creates an immediately recognizable visual language.
